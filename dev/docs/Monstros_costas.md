# Criaturas de costas (5 imagens)

Para o lado do jogador no combate (pose "3/4 de costas", olhando para o canto superior direito). Em cada pedido, **anexe a imagem `ref_costas/referencia_costas_N.png` do mesmo lote** — ela mostra exatamente quais criaturas redesenhar e em que ordem. Mande as imagens na ordem (Costas 1, 2, 3...).

## Costas 1
```
The attached image shows 6 creatures from my game. Redraw the SAME 6 creatures, in the SAME order left to right, keeping exactly the same colors, proportions, markings, accessories and art style (chibi 3D cartoon, glossy toy-like, thick soft dark outline). The ONLY change: turn each creature around into a 3/4 BACK view. We see it from behind and slightly from its left side, as if it is standing in the lower-left of a battlefield looking toward an opponent at the upper-right, away from the camera. Show mostly its back, the back of its head, its tail and wings; only a small sliver of the cheek may be visible, the eyes are NOT visible. Full body, same size relation as in the reference, evenly spaced in ONE row with wide gaps.
Crisp clean sharp edges, fully opaque. Background: solid flat pure magenta (#FF00FF), perfectly uniform. NO smoke, NO glow, NO shadows, NO text.
```
Ordem dos nº: 20151, 20152, 20221, 20222, 20651, 20652
